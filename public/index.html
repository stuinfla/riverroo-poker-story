“Wait.

RiverRoo’s next smart move is to check RuvNet Brain. Why try to invent every part of a poker player if someone in the ecosystem has already built useful pieces?

The research finds rUv’s poker-darwin trainer/solver and the local-logic poker agents. Now there is an existing poker foundation to inspect and build toward.

That is the turning point: stop learning everything the hard way. Use the Brain to discover what already exists, then adapt and test the relevant pieces.

What changedThe discovery changes the build plan: investigate and adapt rUv’s poker work instead of designing the strategy foundation from a blank page.

What the evidence says.

The character scenes are illustrations. Practice counts, software behavior, and test results come from the recovered project and its corrected evaluation.

The early learner

The original learner used uninformed initial action values, 15% random exploration, a 0.08 update rate, 29 coarse hand groups, and fold/call choices. The ruOS records reached 1,020,000 practice hands. These were simplified exercises, not full legal poker.

Information helped. Retrieval delivered it.

Direct case lookup produced identical decisions. The measured benefit belongs to the conditional information and resulting policy; it does not prove that vector retrieval inherently improves poker strategy.

The boundary

Heads-up Texas Hold’em, 10-big-blind preflop shove/fold, stacks reset each hand, no rake, and four synthetic opponents. Big blinds are simulation units, not dollars. Full betting streets, a fully trained full-game bot, and proven live opponent adaptation are not complete.

The RuvNet toolkit

RuOS hosted the experiments. Ruflo coordinated work. RuVector supported case retrieval. Research also located rUv’s poker-darwin solver in ruvnet/metaharness and the poker agents in ruvnet/local-logic. Those existing poker implementations are research foundations; they have not yet been integrated. A separate full-street engine now passes 12 RuOS tests, but trained playing strength remains unproven.
