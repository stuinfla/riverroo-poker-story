What we built, what changed, and what remains.

Current state: a playable full-street prototype; a recovered ten-ability framework; verified memory storage and retrieval; and one bounded offline parameter-calibration improvement against a novice opponent. A broadly strong trained poker player and integrated poker-darwin policy remain unfinished.

Three experiments, not one continuous training history

The early simplified learner, the restricted reference-memory comparison, and the newer full-hand parameter test are separate demonstrations. Their scores use different games, opponents and reward definitions. Do not place them on one learning curve or subtract their results as if they measured the same thing.

The ten-ability framework

Mathematics; game theory; bluffing; opponent psychology; deception; position; tactical opportunism; adaptation; tournament intelligence; metacognition.

These names were recovered from the earlier project’s build prompt and 10 AM checkpoint. No numerical ten-attribute baseline was recorded. Stuart describes their origin as research organised through Ruflo; the original completed research output was not recovered. The story credits that account and exposes the provenance boundary.

The newer full-hand result

The frozen default heuristic averaged 4.65 simulated big blinds per hand; a feedback-selected setting averaged 7.47 against one novice opponent. The paired gain was +2.82, with an approximate 95% interval from +1.14 to +4.50 across 256 unseen seed clusters. Five configurations were compared on 640 training hands before selection. Each test policy played 512 holdout hands.

This is offline parameter selection, not proof of continual learning, expert strength or ten-dimensional improvement. Read the complete method, limits and raw receipts.

The tools’ roles

  • RuOS: remote environment where the engine and experiments executed.
  • Ruflo: work coordination and the research-workflow framing. Tracked tasks alone do not prove autonomous research completion.
  • RuvNet Brain: source research that located existing poker implementations.
  • rUv’s poker-darwin and local-logic agents: discovered foundations for investigation; integration is unfinished.
  • RuVector / RVF: reference retrieval and native persistence. The new RVF file accepted 641 vectors, reopened successfully, and supplied the selected-policy marker for an actual decision. Its independent contribution to performance has not been isolated.

Technical appendix: earlier restricted comparison

The corrected elementary reference-case experiment used 16,000 paired deals in a 10-big-blind preflop shove/fold environment. Its case-minus-uniform difference was +0.02375 big blinds per hand, with an approximate 95% interval of +0.00960 to +0.03790. Direct and vector retrieval made identical decisions. This is not full poker or the new full-hand learning result.
